Meet your practitioner

I’m Iwona.

Registered Nurse & founder

I’m a Registered Nurse specialising in mental health, with over 12 years of nursing experience and a background in clinical leadership and patient care. I opened Amelie Atelier in June 2026 and named it after my daughter, Amelia.

I wanted to create a calm, private space where you can talk openly about your concerns and feel comfortable asking questions. I take the time to understand what you’re looking for, assess your skin and talk you through the options so you can decide what feels right for you.
